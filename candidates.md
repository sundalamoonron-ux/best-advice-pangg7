# Kandidat — The Best Advice I Ever Got (u-pangg7-the-best-advice-i-ever-got-358cefb0)

Brief juri (ringkas): 1 kalimat, nasihat yang benar-benar pernah diberikan seseorang;
paling on-subject, paling earned/spesifik, suara nyata (bukan slogan/klise), ketat, dan
landing. Maks 200 karakter, UTF-8, bukan spasi saja.

## Kandidat (10 varian)

1. My father told me, 'Never borrow money to look rich,' and twenty years later I still check my bank balance before my ego.
2. My grandmother said, 'Cook for the people you love like they'll remember the meal,' and I have never rushed a dinner since.
3. The night I wanted to quit, my coach said, 'You don't have to be the best, you just have to still be here tomorrow,' and I was.
4. My mother told me, 'Marry someone who is kind when nobody is watching,' and that one sentence has saved me more than once.
5. My first boss said, 'Nobody remembers how fast you finished, they remember whether it worked,' and I stopped racing the clock.
6. My grandfather told me, 'A man who can't fix his own fence shouldn't buy a bigger house,' and I still hear it every time I want more.
7. When I lost my first job, my father said, 'A door closes, but a window opens — go find the window,' and I did.
8. My teacher said, 'Write it down, because the brain forgets what the heart meant to keep,' and my notebooks have never been empty since.
9. My mentor told me, 'Say yes to the work nobody wants, because that's where the learning hides,' and every promotion since traced back to it.
10. My father said, 'If you can't explain it to your grandmother, you don't understand it yet,' and I have rewritten everything twice since.

## Uji pairwise (kriteria juri, urutan 1-5)

- #7 kalah dari semua: "door closes, window opens" adalah klise yang sudah dikenal — gagal kriteria 2 & 3.
- #2 kalah dari #1: hangat tapi nasihatnya ("cook like they'll remember") kurang tajam/spesifik.
- #4 kalah dari #1: "kind when nobody is watching" adalah aforisme yang sudah beredar — terasa fortune-cookie.
- #5 kalah dari #1: "nobody remembers how fast" adalah kebijaksanaan korporat yang generik.
- #8 kalah dari #1: "brain forgets what the heart meant to keep" terdengar dibuat-buat/puitis, bukan ucapan orang sungguhan.
- #9 kalah dari #1: "say yes to the work nobody wants" adalah nasihat karier generik.
- Finalis: #1 vs #3 — #1 menang: lebih spesifik & mengejutkan ("borrow money to look rich"), landing lebih kuat ("check my bank balance before my ego"); #3 ("still be here tomorrow") lebih dekat ke ketekunan generik.
- Finalis: #1 vs #6 — #1 menang tipis: nasihat #6 agak samar, landing #1 langsung bisa dipakai.
- Finalis: #1 vs #10 — #1 menang: #10 lebih seperti tips produktivitas; #1 terasa seperti nasihat hidup dari ayah dengan konsekuensi nyata.
- #3 vs #6 — #3 menang tipis (lebih emosional, lebih landing). #6 vs #10 — #6 menang (lebih banyak suara, brief: kepintaran demi kepintaran tidak dihitung).

## Hasil

Pemenang: #1 — 3 menang, 0 kalah di babak finalis (vs #3, #6, #10).
Runner-up: #3 (coach, "still be here tomorrow").
